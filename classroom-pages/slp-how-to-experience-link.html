How to experience SLP

You do not have to consume SLP like a normal course. Use it according to the leadership pressure you are carrying.

3 paths

Choose the route that fits the pressure.

The point is not to finish fast. The point is to think better, apply sooner, and know when the pressure needs a thinking partner.

01
Deep Thinking Path

Watch. Pause. Work the case.

Watch the classes, pause between heavier sections, complete the exercises, and let the case sharpen how you read pressure, people, trade-offs, and timing.

02
Apply Playbook Path

Go straight to application.

If you already have a live leadership challenge, go straight into the SLP Companion and use the tools to map your own scenario.

03
SLP Coaching Path

Use a thinking partner.

If the pressure is complex, high-stakes, or hard to untangle alone, Janine offers coaching to pressure-test your thinking and turn insight into action.
